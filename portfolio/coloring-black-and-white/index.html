Coloring black and white

Home / Collaboration projects / Coloring black and white

Shades of black, levitating factures and captivating lines of Angela_A helped us to encapsulate bold, mysterious and sensual Pyrenees. Little crazy spark, that ignited between us and Angelina pushed us to take her brand to the French/Spanish border, and create series of photographs that will keep the spirit of those places.

From free-loving Catalonia to the tough country of Basks, from high mountain passes to french balcony roads - we drove through almost every corner of Pyrenees trying to capture brightest moments of this journey. With every set in this location we had minutes of joy, surprise, unease to remember, but instead of telling you all of that, we tried to express it through the lens of the camera.

In Pyrenees we've seen a wide variety of colors, nevertheless this part of our work will remain black&white. It is this type of photography that transmits the atmosphere of down falling fog and sliding light, that inspired us the most.

In counter to balance black&white photography, colorful brushstrokes of nature in fashion photography with clothes by Angel_A pushes the specific image to the front, the image created by this designer, how you feel when you try this image on.

This is not our first attempt to create new way of sharing our travel. Fashion-landscape photography is the best way for us to show what we see when driving these places by, but also which image we want to pass, founding ourselves there, where something touches the strings of our inspiration.

Don't be scared to travel, to uncover new in the world, in yourself and in fashion of course!
